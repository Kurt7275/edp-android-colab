Place your greeting_screenshot.png in this folder.

The screenshot must show:
- The app running on the Greeting screen
- The text: "Hello, <your name>! Welcome to Jetpack Navigation."

Steps to take the screenshot:
1. Run the app on your emulator (press the green ▶ button in Android Studio)
2. Type your name on the Home screen
3. Tap "Show Greeting"
4. On the emulator sidebar, click the camera 📷 icon
5. Save it as "greeting_screenshot.png" in this folder
6. Then commit and push!
